# Glossary

**Archive:** Data retained for a business, legal, or historical reason and accessed less often than active data.

**Data owner:** The accountable business role that decides purpose, classification, retention, access, and deletion.

**Data steward:** The operational role that maintains metadata and helps the owner apply policy.

**Lifecycle rule:** An S3 configuration that transitions or expires objects based on age, prefix, or tags.

**Legal hold:** A direction to preserve data despite a normal expiry date.

**RPO / RTO:** Recovery Point Objective (how much data loss is tolerable) and Recovery Time Objective (how quickly recovery must complete).

**Retention class:** A named policy bundle containing transition, expiry, restore, and control requirements.

**Restore:** Making archived data readable again, possibly after an asynchronous S3 restore request.

**System of record:** The authoritative source from which data correctness is established.

**Unassigned dataset:** A dataset with no accepted accountable owner. It should not be automatically deleted.
